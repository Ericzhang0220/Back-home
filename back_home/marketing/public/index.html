BEFORE YOU COME IN

A few questions.

What is Back Home?

Back Home is an app built around your personal room, daily mood check-ins, music, and conversations. It’s a place to make time for yourself and connect at your own pace.

What can I do in my room?

Personalize your room with furniture, change the arrangement, and enjoy background music. Make it a place you look forward to returning to.

Who can I chat with?

The app includes conversations with friends and AI companions. AI companions are identified as AI, and the Hall offers a place to share posts and discover other people.

Where can I download the app?

A public download link isn’t available on this page yet. Once the app’s store listing is ready, you’ll be able to find it here.
